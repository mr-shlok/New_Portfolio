ABOUT SHLOK JADHAV

Software Development Engineer at Accelya Services India · Information Technology Graduate

I'm Shlok Jadhav, an Information Technology graduate and Software Development Engineer at Accelya Services India Private Limited, focused on building reliable, scalable, and thoughtfully engineered software solutions.

My technical foundation lies in Java and full-stack development, with hands-on experience across object-oriented programming, Spring Boot, REST APIs, SQL, database integration, and application development. I enjoy understanding how systems work beneath the surface — from designing backend logic and managing data to connecting components into complete, functional applications.

My journey has been shaped by internships, independent projects, hackathons, and competitive technical challenges. These experiences have strengthened my problem-solving abilities, collaborative mindset, and approach to translating ideas into working solutions.

Beyond code, my experience in client relations and competitive debating has helped me develop communication, adaptability, and the ability to approach challenges from different perspectives.

I believe good engineering is more than writing code. It is about making deliberate decisions, building with purpose, and continuously improving the way technology serves people.
